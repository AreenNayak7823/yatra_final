✦ Unique Feature

Tourist Visa Overstay Prevention System

A critical national security and tourism management feature — unique to Yatra

🛂
Why This Matters
Every year, thousands of tourists enter India on a valid Tourist Visa but remain after expiry. Yatra's backend (Node.js + MongoDB) monitors every registered tourist's visa status in real-time and sends automated email alerts — protecting both the tourist and India's borders. Overstay data is reported directly to FRRO via our Admin Dashboard.
